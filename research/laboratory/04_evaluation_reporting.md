# Stage 04, Evaluation & Reporting

**Status:** Not started, no integration decisions approved.

## Purpose

Assess what the research proves, what remains uncertain and which requirements a future KINORYX implementation would need to meet.

## Review criteria

- Traceability to the original source recording and acquisition metadata.
- Correctness of calculations, parameter sensitivity and unit handling.
- Reproducibility of visualizations and summaries.
- Scientific validity of physiological statements and time alignment.
- Usability of comparative workspaces, documentation and export.
- Separating the independent Qualisys reference study from Cometa/Xsens recordings.

## Deliverables

A reproducible report, accepted/rejected hypotheses and an explicit engineering decision log. See [DECISION_LOG.md](../DECISION_LOG.md) for future proposals.

**No functionality is approved for main simply because it appears on this experimental study branch.**
